Broadcast Spy · M5StickC

The easy spy for the first round. It runs on the stick itself — screen and two buttons, no computer attached. Capture a command off the air and send it right back.

Attacker · Round 1 only

What it does

While the race runs in the clear on broadcast, this spy hears every command the wands send and lists them on its screen. Pick one and it replays it — and the car obeys the impostor.

Button A — replay the selected command
Button B — move the selection through the captured history

When it stops working

This spy only ever hears broadcast traffic. The moment the race switches to unicast in round 2, the wands address the car directly and this stick goes quiet — it never sees the commands again.

That dead end is the point of the round: addressing the message hid it from the simple spy. To keep attacking, the attacker has to move up to the monitor spy, which listens to the whole channel.

Flash it

  1. Find the stick's port and copy the program as main.py: 
    mpremote connect <port> fs cp micropython/espiao/espiao.py :main.py
  2. The screen driver (the m5/ folder) already lives on the board and is not in the repo. Back it up once, in case you reflash: 
    mpremote connect <port> fs cp -r :m5 micropython/espiao/
  3. Power the stick from a battery. It boots straight into ESPIAO … listening and starts filling its history as the wands drive.
