Command reference

    

      
k<n>Tune the spy to channel n (1–13). You only hear and can only replay on the channel you are on — pick your target car's channel.

      
lList the captured packets (newest 40).

      
<n>Replay packet #n once — the car obeys the impostor.

      
<n> x<K>Replay packet #n K times (1–20, 300 ms apart). Flood STOP and the car stalls — the availability attack.

      
uReplay the last packet seen.

      
mToggle live printing of captures as they arrive.

      
cClear the history.

      
hHelp.

      
BOOTThe BOOT button on the board also replays the last packet.
